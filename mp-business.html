Offer architecture

  1. Pick a niche.
  2. Name the painful problem.
  3. Define one measurable outcome.
  4. Build a simple offer around it.
  5. Generate leads on purpose.
  6. Close honestly. Deliver. Collect proof.
  7. Turn the whole thing into a repeatable checklist.

Client discovery questions

  • What are you doing now?
  • What isn't working?
  • What does that actually cost you?
  • What would success look like?
  • Why deal with it now?
  • What have you already tried?
